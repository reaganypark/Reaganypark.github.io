Vacation in the United States

California

California is a state on the western side of the US that borders the Pacific Ocean. Its neighbors are Oregon on top, Mexico on the bottom, and Nevada and Arizona to the east. You can go visit the many miles of beaches, Disneyland, explore cities like Los Angeles and San Francisco, or enjoy the beautiful national parks. California also has great food and shopping places all across the state. With so many different activites, California is a fun place for everyone.
